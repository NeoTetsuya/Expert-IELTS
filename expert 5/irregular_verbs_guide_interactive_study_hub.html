Pattern Recognition for Easy Memorization

Group irregular verbs by their vowel and structural shifts!

Group 1 • A-A-A 🔒

Zero Change Forms

Infinitive, Past Simple, and Past Participle are identical.

broadcast • cost • cut • hit • hurt • let • put • read* • set • shut • spread *Note: 'read' changes pronunciation (/riːd/ → /red/) but keeps spelling.
Group 2 • A-B-B 🧠

-ought / -aught Shift

Past Simple and Participle end in -ought or -aught.

bring → brought → brought
buy → bought → bought
catch → caught → caught
fight → fought → fought
teach → taught → taught
think → thought → thought
Group 3 • A-B-C 🎵

i → a → u Vowel Sequence

Classic 3-step vowel change from i to a to u.

begin → began → begun
drink → drank → drunk
ring → rang → rung
sing → sang → sung
swim → swam → swum
Group 4 • A-B-C ⭐

Past Participle ends in -en

Common pattern where past participle adds -en or -n.

break → broke → broken • choose → chose → chosen
drive → drove → driven • eat → ate → eaten
fall → fell → fallen • forget → forgot → forgotten
freeze → froze → frozen • give → gave → given
speak → spoke → spoken • steal → stole → stolen
take → took → taken • write → wrote → written
