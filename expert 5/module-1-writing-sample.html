Expert writing

Task IELTS Writing Prompt

Write about the following topic:

"Many students choose to study in a different country from their own.

What are the advantages and disadvantages of studying abroad?"

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

Model answer & Method Annotations

254 words • Band 7.5+ Sample calibrated for Band 5 mastery
Essay Planning Strategy

Plan your essay before you start writing. Note down ideas and make a paragraph plan.

💡 Plan 4 clean paragraphs: Intro → Advantages (Language & Culture) → Disadvantages (Loneliness & Food) → Conclusion.
Topic Sentences

Make the first sentence of each body paragraph a topic sentence.

⭐ Body 1: "The main advantage of studying abroad is..."
Body 2: "But studying abroad is not always easy."
Concrete Examples

Use personal anecdotes or specific situations to make abstract points relatable.

💡 "My cousin who did a degree in the USA felt very lonely for his first term or two..."
Paragraph 1: Introduction (Prompt Paraphrase & Thesis)

Nowadays a lot of students decide to go to college or university abroad. There are many reasons why this is a sensible decision, but being a student in a foreign country can also sometimes be difficult.

Paragraph 2: Body 1 (Key Advantages - Language, Employment & Culture)

The main advantage of studying abroad is that the student not only gets a degree in, say, engineering or medicine, but also becomes fluent in a foreign language. Knowing another language is always useful but it can be a particular plus when the student starts looking for a job. A second advantage is that a student who does a course in a foreign country gains an understanding of a completely different culture from his or her own. This is valuable in many different ways. It usually makes the student more tolerant of other ways of looking at the world and it also introduces them to new singers and writers.

Paragraph 3: Body 2 (Disadvantages - Homesickness & Initial Culture Shock)

But studying abroad is not always easy. My cousin who did a degree in the USA felt very lonely for his first term or two. He missed his family and friends and, to begin with, he did not like the food or the way of life there. Gradually, however, he got used to being in Texas and by the end of the course he was sorry to leave.

Paragraph 4: Conclusion (Summary & Final Evaluation)

To sum up, doing a degree or some other course in a foreign country can be hard but, for most students, it offers more advantages than disadvantages and so is a very positive thing to consider if you have the opportunity to do so.

Introduction Paraphrase

Paraphrase the statement from the writing task in your introduction.

💡 Replaces: "choose to study in a different country" with "decide to go to college or university abroad".
Clear Paragraphing

Write in clear paragraphs.

⭐ Maintain a distinct paragraph for advantages and a separate paragraph for disadvantages to secure Band 6+ Coherence.
Review & Edit

Remember to re-read and check your essay carefully.

✏️ Check that your conclusion clearly summarizes whether advantages outweigh disadvantages.

Advantages Frame

The main advantage of [X] is that...

Why this helps Band 5 Learners:

Gives your advantages body paragraph an immediate, clean topic sentence so the examiner instantly recognises your main idea.

Band 5 → Band 6+ Upgrade Example:

"The primary benefit of pursuing higher education overseas is linguistic immersion."

Band 5 Advantages-Disadvantages Toolkit (Module 1)

1. Advantages-Disadvantages Essay Skeleton

Introduction:

Nowadays a lot of students decide to [paraphrase prompt topic]. There are many reasons why this is a sensible decision, but [topic] can also sometimes be difficult.

Body 1 (Advantages):

The main advantage of [X] is that the student not only [Advantage 1A: get a degree], but also [Advantage 1B: language fluency]. A second advantage is that [Advantage 2: cultural understanding]. This makes students more tolerant.

Body 2 (Disadvantages & Anecdote):

But [X] is not always easy. My [relative/friend] who [action] felt very lonely to begin with and missed family and food. Gradually, however, he got used to it and was sorry to leave.

Conclusion:

To sum up, [topic] can be hard but, for most students, it offers more advantages than disadvantages and is a very positive thing to consider.
