Module 10

Task 2: Opinion essay

Expert writing

• There are two parts to this statement that you need to think about: English becoming more widely spoken and other languages dying out.

• You must say if you agree or disagree with the statement and give reasons.

Task IELTS Writing Prompt

Write about the following topic:

"Soon almost everyone in the world will speak English and most other languages will die out.

To what extent do you agree with this point of view?"

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

Model answer & Method Annotations

264 words • Band 7.5+ Sample calibrated for Band 5 mastery
Stance Placement

You can start your first body paragraph by stating your opinion.

💡 "I partly agree with their opinion. It is clearly true that English is becoming widely spoken all over the world."
Two-Part Question Balance

Start a new paragraph to discuss the second part of the statement.

⭐ Para 2 covers English expansion; Para 3 addresses whether native languages will disappear.
Conclusion Strategy

State your opinion in the conclusion. Synthesize both perspectives into a clear final judgment.

Paragraph 1: Introduction (Prompt Paraphrase)

Some people say that it will not be long before English is spoken everywhere in the world. They believe that the majority of other languages will become extinct.

Paragraph 2: Body 1 (Agreed Part - Rise of Global English)

I partly agree with their opinion. It is clearly true that English is becoming widely spoken all over the world. Everywhere you go now you see English. Even in countries where English is not the native language, you often find that menus, tourist brochures and street signs are in English. Many international businesses use English in their offices even when those offices are in Switzerland, Delhi or Hong Kong. When a Chinese person meets a Brazilian, they will probably talk to each other in English rather than Chinese or Portuguese. That is the situation now and I think English will become even more important in the next few years.

Paragraph 3: Body 2 (Disagreed Part - Cultural Value of Native Tongues)

However, I do not think that most other languages will disappear. In my opinion, people will still speak their native languages when they are at home. Most people are proud of their own language. They want to pass it on to the next generation. A language is not just words — it is also stories and songs and poetry. Parents will always want to share these aspects of their language culture with their children and they will want schools to teach their children their own language as well as English.

Paragraph 4: Conclusion (Balanced Synthesis)

In conclusion, I agree that English will become more and more important as the international language of business and travel. But I do not believe that this will lead to the death of other languages.

Exemplification

Give examples to help make your ideas clear.

💡 Menus and street signs; offices in Switzerland, Delhi, and Hong Kong; Chinese person talking to a Brazilian.
Language of Opinion

Use the language of opinion: • I partly agree with... • It is clearly true that... • In my opinion... • I do not believe that...

Exam Time Management

Allow time to check your work carefully. Reserve 3–5 minutes at the end to correct spelling, subject-verb agreement, and punctuation.

Module 10 • Task 2: Opinion essay 209
Opinion Device

I partly agree with their opinion...

Why this helps Band 5 Learners:

Enables you to handle prompts with two separate claims without writing a confusing all-or-nothing response.

Band 5 → Band 6+ Upgrade Example:

"While I concur that English dominates international business, I strongly disagree that regional languages will become extinct."

Band 5 Two-Part Opinion Toolkit (Module 10)

1. Two-Part Opinion Essay Skeleton (Balanced Stance)

Introduction:

Some people say that [Part 1: English spread]. They believe that [Part 2: other languages will become extinct].

Body 1 (Agreed Part - English in Global Sphere):

I partly agree with their opinion. It is clearly true that English is becoming widely spoken. Everywhere you go, you see [Examples: signs, business offices, international meetings]. I think English will become even more important.

Body 2 (Disagreed Part - Heritage & Home Life):

However, I do not think that [languages will disappear]. In my opinion, people will still speak native languages at home. Most people are proud of their language and want to pass it on to the next generation because [stories/culture].

Conclusion:

In conclusion, I agree that English will become more important as the language of business, but I do not believe this will lead to the death of other languages.
