Model answer & Method Annotations

172 words • Band 7.5+ Sample calibrated for Band 5 mastery
Introduction Strategy

Introduce the topic of the graph. Try to paraphrase the original sentence.

💡 Replaces: "number of social networking sites people used" with "number of social networking sites visited by internet users in the USA".
Key Features & Figures

Write about the main features. Mention some of the most significant figures shown in the chart.

⭐ Quotes the major categories: "just over one fifth (22% to 21%)" and the highest group ("One site: 36% down to 28%").
Cohesion & Contrast Linkers

Linking words and phrases like in contrast help to show the connection between the sentences or paragraphs.

Paragraph 1: Introduction & Overall Trend Overview

The bar chart shows the number of social networking sites visited by internet users in the USA in 2013 and in 2014. We can see from the data that, in general, there was a growing tendency to use more than one site.

Paragraph 2: Main Features (Static Non-Users & Peak Single-Site Drop)

Although just over one fifth of internet users did not use any social networking sites in either of the years, the proportion in this category decreased from 22 percent in 2013 to 21 percent in 2014. In both years the highest proportion of users in any group fell into the \'One site\' category. However, this group declined sharply from 36 percent in 2013 to 28 percent in 2014.

Paragraph 3: Multiple Sites Growth & Trend Verbs Variety

In contrast, the numbers who reported using two sites, three sites, four sites and five sites all grew over the period analysed. In both years the proportion of internet users fell as the number of social networking sites increased, with only 2 percent using five sites in 2013 compared to 4 percent in 2014.

Overview Requirement

Include an overview, describing the main trend.

⚠️ Band 5 → 6 rule: Without an overview statement, Task Achievement cannot exceed Band 5.
Trend Vocabulary Variety

Use a variety of verbs: • grow / grew • fall / fell • increase / increased • decrease / decreased • decline sharply

Comparative Precision

Use fractions and percentages alternately: just over one fifth alongside 22 percent.

Task 1 Formula

Gives your Task 1 an immediate, crystal-clear overview sentence to secure Band 6 in Task Achievement.

Band 5 → Band 6+ Upgrade Example:

"Overall, it is evident that internet users exhibited an increasing inclination towards utilizing multiple platforms simultaneously."

Band 5 Bar Chart Toolkit (Module 2)

1. Bar Chart Report Skeleton (Two Time Periods)

Introduction & Overview:

The bar chart shows [paraphrase prompt topic] in [Location] in [Year 1] and [Year 2]. We can see from the data that, in general, there was a [growing/falling tendency to X].

Body 1 (Dominant Categories & Drops):

Although [fraction/proportion] did not [action], the proportion decreased from [X]% to [Y]%. In both years the highest proportion fell into the [Group] category. However, this group declined sharply from [X]% to [Y]%.

Body 2 (Rising Categories & Direct Comparison):

In contrast, the numbers who [actions] all grew over the period analysed. In both years the proportion fell as the number increased, with only [X]% in [Year 1] compared to [Y]% in [Year 2].
