Task 2: Problem-solution essay

Expert writing

The problem: What you have to write about.

• Write about at least two problems.

• Suggest at least one solution for each of the problems you mention.

Task IELTS Writing Prompt

Write about the following topic:

"Some young people decide to get a job straight after leaving school instead of going to university.

What problems might this decision cause them? What can they do to solve these problems?"

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

Model answer & Method Annotations

254 words • Band 7.5+ Sample calibrated for Band 5 mastery
Introduction Strategy

Paraphrase the statement from the writing task in your introduction.

💡 Replaces: "get a job straight after leaving school" with "earn a salary as soon as they possibly can... take the decision not to go to university"
Solution & Justification

Give reasons to explain why your solutions are appropriate.

💡 "In that way, it is possible to gain qualifications and earn money at the same time."
Exemplification

Give examples to explain your ideas (e.g. personal experience or specific jobs).

⭐ "That is the best way to become, for example, a mechanic or an electrician."
Paragraph 1: Introduction (Paraphrase & Outline)

When they leave school, many students feel that they need to earn a salary as soon as they possibly can. They therefore take the decision not to go to university. This decision may be the right one for them but it can cause problems.

Paragraph 2: Problem 1 (Low Qualifications & Pay) + Solutions

The first problem is that it is hard for a school-leaver with few qualifications to find a job with a good salary. One solution is to take work but continue studying in the evenings and at weekends. In that way, it is possible to gain qualifications and earn money at the same time. Once the person has got more qualifications, he or she can start looking for a better job. Another solution is to find work where it is possible to learn useful skills on the job. That is the best way to become, for example, a mechanic or an electrician.

Paragraph 3: Problem 2 (Lifestyle & Social Shock) + Solution

Another problem in going straight to work after leaving school is that it can be hard to get down to a serious job starting work at nine and finishing at five, five days a week, when friends are living a more relaxed and independent life at university. I know my brother found that very difficult when he got his first job when he was eighteen. He solved the problem by making sure he had a good social life. He formed a band and they had a lot of fun playing together every weekend.

Paragraph 4: Conclusion (Summary & Final Stance)

In conclusion, although there are problems going to work straight after leaving school, it is not too difficult to find solutions to these problems.

Conclusion Strategy

Summarise your point of view in your conclusion.

⚠️ Band 5 requirement: Re-state that while challenges exist, practical solutions can overcome them.
Structure Rule

Problem-Solution Pairing: • Body 1: Problem 1 + Solutions (Work & Study / On-the-job training) • Body 2: Problem 2 + Solution (Social life balance)

Signposting Key

• The first problem is that...
• One solution is to...
• Another solution is to...
• Another problem is that...

Module 3 • Problem-solution essay 202
Sentence Frame

The first problem is that...

Why this helps Band 5 Learners:

Provides a clear and foolproof structure to introduce a major issue without making grammatical mistakes.

Band 5 → Band 6+ Upgrade Example:

"The primary problem is that young school-leavers lack practical job experience."

Band 5 Problem-Solution Toolkit

1. Essential Essay Skeleton

Introduction:

When they [paraphrase prompt situation], many people feel that [topic]. This decision can cause problems, but solutions can be found.

Body 1 (Problem 1 + Solutions):

The first problem is that [Problem 1]. One solution is to [Solution 1A]. In that way, it is possible to [Positive result]. Another solution is to [Solution 1B]. That is the best way to [Example].

Body 2 (Problem 2 + Solution):

Another problem in [Topic action] is that [Problem 2]. [Person/People] can solve the problem by [Solution 2].

Conclusion:

In conclusion, although there are problems [verb-ing], it is not too difficult to find solutions to these problems.

  • gain qualifications get certificates/degrees
