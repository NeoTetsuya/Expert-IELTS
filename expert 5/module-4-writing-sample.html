Module 4

Task 2: Opinion essay

Expert writing tip

You need to decide which side is stronger — the advantages or the disadvantages — and explain your opinion clearly throughout.

Task IELTS Writing Prompt

Write about the following topic:

"Some people believe it is wrong to keep animals in zoos.

Do you think the advantages of keeping animals in zoos outweigh the disadvantages?"

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

Model answer & Method Annotations

262 words • Band 7.5+ Sample calibrated for Band 5 mastery
Introduction Strategy

Introduce the topic by explaining it in your own words (paraphrasing the prompt).

💡 Replaces: "it is wrong to keep animals" with "cruel and unnecessary to keep animals in captivity"
Signposting / Cohesion

Use phrases like: • The main advantage is... • A further advantage is...

⭐ Guarantees clear paragraph progression for Coherence & Cohesion (Band 5 → 6).
Supporting Ideas

Give examples to support the points you make.

💡 "Children in Europe, for example, can see elephants..."
Concluding Phrase

"In conclusion" is the safest, most useful phrase to introduce your final paragraph.

Paragraph 1: Introduction

Some people argue that it is cruel and unnecessary to keep animals in captivity. In my opinion, although zoos have both good and bad points, the advantages are greater than the disadvantages.

Paragraph 2: Advantage 1 (Conservation) & Advantage 2 (Education)

The main advantage of zoos is that they help to protect some animals that are in danger of becoming extinct. They give them the chance to produce young and to survive in a safe environment. This means that future generations will be able to enjoy looking at and learning about more of the wonderful animals that live on our planet.

A further advantage is that zoos make it possible to see animals that come from very different places. Children in Europe, for example, can see elephants and giraffes and children in Brazil can enjoy watching kangaroos and emus. I believe this is a very special and important experience for young people.

Paragraph 3: Counter-argument (Disadvantages & Rebuttal)

However, there are disadvantages to zoos. The main one is that the animals that live there are not free. They are not living in a totally natural way, able to go where they want when they want. Many people believe that it is not right to keep animals in this kind of prison.

But you can also argue that the natural world is a cruel place for wild animals. It is hard for them to find food and they are likely to be attacked by other animals. They may well live longer in a zoo than in their natural environment.

Paragraph 4: Conclusion

In conclusion, I am in favour of zoos. I believe the benefits they bring us are more important than any disadvantages.

Clear Position

Summarise your own view clearly in the introduction.

⚠️ Band 5 requirement: The examiner must see your opinion from the start, not just at the end.
Elaboration & Relevance

Explain the main point in more detail and give examples where possible. Make sure all the information is relevant.

Essay Weighting

Write more about the side that you think is stronger.

💡 Here, advantages = 2 points + examples (105 words); disadvantages = counter-balanced (75 words).
Conclusion Strategy

Give your opinion in the conclusion, summarising the reasons and directly answering whether advantages outweigh disadvantages.

Module 4 • Opinion Essay Writing 203
Linking Phrase

The main advantage of [X] is that...

Why this helps Band 5 Learners:

Gives your paragraph an immediate, crystal-clear topic sentence so the examiner immediately knows your main idea.

Band 5 → Band 6 Upgrade Example:

"The main advantage of public transport is that it reduces traffic jams in big cities."

Band 5 Essential Toolkit

1. Essential Essay Skeleton (Band 5)

Introduction:

Some people argue that [paraphrase prompt]. In my opinion, although [topic] has both good and bad points, the advantages are greater than the disadvantages.

Body 1 (Advantages):

The main advantage of [X] is that [Reason 1]. This means that [Result]. A further advantage is that [Reason 2]. For example, [Example].

Body 2 (Disadvantages):

However, there are disadvantages to [X]. The main one is that [Disadvantage]. But you can also argue that [Counter-rebuttal].

Conclusion:

In conclusion, I am in favour of [X]. I believe the benefits they bring us are more important than any disadvantages.
