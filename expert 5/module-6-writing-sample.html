Task 2: Opinion essay

Expert writing

• You must say if you agree or disagree with the statement and give reasons.

• You can invent some examples if you cannot think of any from your own experience.

💡 If you have no strong feelings about the topic, remember you do not have to tell the truth. Choose a possible point of view and argue it as well as you can.

Task IELTS Writing Prompt

Write about the following topic:

"People these days give too much importance to following fashions, for example wearing fashionable clothes and having the latest model of phone.

To what extent do you agree with this statement?"

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

Model answer & Method Annotations

268 words • Band 7.5+ Sample calibrated for Band 5 mastery
Reason & Result Linkers

Use linking words like because, as and as a result to explain and give reasons for the points you make.

💡 "because I often see it with my own eyes..." • "...as a result of reading about them in the press."
Evidence & Elaboration

Explain and support the points you made in previous paragraphs.

⭐ Moves from personal circles (friend upgrading phone, sister's clothes) to broader media impact (serious debt from credit card shopping).
Balanced Perspective

Acknowledge common human desires before stating your core moral opinion: "Of course, everyone likes... but..."

Paragraph 1: Introduction (Prompt Paraphrase)

A lot of people these days spend a great deal of money keeping up with fashion. They always want to have the newest electronic gadget and the latest style of shoes.

Paragraph 2: Personal Evidence (Friends & Family)

I believe this to be the case because I often see it with my own eyes. The people I know love to keep up with the latest fashions. My best friend, for example, always wants to upgrade his phone as soon as a new model is released. My sister hates wearing clothes that were fashionable several years ago because she says they look out of date now.

Paragraph 3: Societal Aspect (Media Pressure & Debt)

It is not just people that I know. I often read articles in a newspaper or magazine describing how someone is in serious debt because they bought some expensive new clothes or the latest design of television or oven on their credit card. The media criticise people who get into money problems but the press is itself partly to blame. People only want fashionable things as a result of reading about them in the press.

Paragraph 4: Conclusion (Value Shift & Final Thought)

Of course, everyone likes having nice possessions but I think being fashionable has too much importance these days. It would be better to think more about relationships with other people and less about material goods. In the end, we get more pleasure from our friends and our family than we do from having the latest fridge or handbag. If our clothes are comfortable, what does it really matter if they are a few years old?

Introduction Strategy

Try to put the topic in your own words in the introduction.

💡 Paraphrases "following fashions" with "spend a great deal of money keeping up with fashion" and "newest electronic gadget".
Paragraph Structure

Make sure each paragraph deals with a distinct aspect of the topic.

⭐ Para 2 = Personal circle & habits.
Para 3 = Commercial media & debt.
Stylistic Device

A question can be an effective way to begin or end an essay.

❓ "If our clothes are comfortable, what does it really matter if they are a few years old?"

Linking Device

I believe this to be the case because...

Why this helps Band 5 Learners:

Provides a smooth and natural way to link your opinion to concrete reasons without repeating "I think that".

Band 5 → Band 6+ Upgrade Example:

"I believe this trend is growing because advertising heavily influences teenagers."

Band 5 Opinion Essay Toolkit (Module 6)

1. Essay Skeleton (Agreement & Reasons)

Introduction:

A lot of people these days [paraphrase prompt topic]. They always want to have [concrete modern examples].

Body 1 (Personal & Immediate Examples):

I believe this to be the case because I often see it with my own eyes. My [friend/relative], for example, always wants to [habit] because [reason].

Body 2 (Broader Aspect / Media & Society):

It is not just people that I know. I often read articles describing how [societal impact/debt] because of [behavior]. People do this as a result of [media/advertising].

Conclusion:

Of course, everyone likes [nice things] but I think [topic] has too much importance. In the end, we get more pleasure from [relationships] than from [material goods]. [Rhetorical ending question]?
