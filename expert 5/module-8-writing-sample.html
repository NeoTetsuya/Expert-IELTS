Task 2: Problem-solution essay

Expert writing

• This is the problem you need to consider.

• There are two parts to the question: reasons for the problem and solutions to the problem.

Task IELTS Writing Prompt

Write about the following topic:

"Many adults nowadays do no physical activity in their free time.

Why do you think this is happening? What can we do to solve the problem?"

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

Model answer & Method Annotations

265 words • Band 7.5+ Sample calibrated for Band 5 mastery
Introduction Strategy

Introduce the problem in your own words.

💡 Replaces: "Many adults do no physical activity in free time" with "It is a sad fact that many people today do no sport or other physical exercise in their spare time."
Topic Sentences & Body

Begin your main body paragraphs with a topic sentence. Write two or three main body paragraphs.

⭐ Para 2: Entertainment distractions.
Para 3: Work fatigue & commute.
Para 4: School guidance & public funding.
Solution Phrases

Use appropriate phrases to refer to solutions: • find a solution • provide a solution • one solution might be • another possible solution

Paragraph 1: Introduction (Problem Stance)

It is a sad fact that many people today do no sport or other physical exercise in their spare time.

Paragraph 2: Cause 1 (Modern Temptations & Screen Time)

One reason for this is that there are so many other tempting things for people to do. At home there are hundreds of different TV channels to watch, there are lots of exciting computer games to play or you can easily spend hours on social networking sites. If you are out, there are cafés, cinemas and shops to keep you away from the gym.

Paragraph 3: Cause 2 (Overwork & Exhaustion)

A further reason is that people have to work very long hours these days. The journey to work is also longer and more stressful than it was for most people in the past. As a result, people do not have enough energy to go for a run or a game of tennis when they eventually get home from work.

Paragraph 4: Solutions (Education & Public Sports Funding)

It will not be easy to find a solution to this problem. I think schools have a key role to play. Young children need to learn how important and how enjoyable physical activity is. In addition, teachers should help each student find a sport they can enjoy. Not everyone likes football or tennis, for example; some will find swimming or athletics more fun. Funding can also help provide a solution. The government should fund sports centres to make it possible for everyone to participate in sport even if they do not have much money.

Paragraph 5: Conclusion (Summary of Urgency & Impact)

Lack of activity is a serious problem for the health of many people, but the solutions suggested above could do a great deal to help solve it.

Cohesion & Linking

Use linking words and phrases.

💡 "One reason for this is...", "A further reason is...", "As a result", "In addition", "for example".
Elaborating Solutions

Explain how your solutions might help.

⭐ Shows the mechanism: funding sports centres makes exercise accessible to low-income adults.
Review & Edit Checklist

Re-read your essay. Check that your ideas are organised well. Then check your use of language and your spelling.

Module 8 • Task 2: Problem-solution essay 207
Problem-Solution Frame

One reason for this is that...

Why this helps Band 5 Learners:

Gives your cause paragraph an immediate, clean topic sentence to avoid fragmented thoughts.

Band 5 → Band 6+ Upgrade Example:

"One primary reason for this inactivity is the increasing prevalence of sedentary desk jobs."

Band 5 Problem-Solution Toolkit (Module 8)

1. Problem-Solution Essay Skeleton (Causes & Solutions)

Introduction:

It is a sad fact that [paraphrase problem topic] in their [spare/free time].

Body 1 (Cause 1 - Distractions/Lifestyle):

One reason for this is that [Cause 1]. At home there are [sedentary entertainment examples]. If people are out, there are [distractions] to keep them away from [healthy activity].

Body 2 (Cause 2 - Work & Fatigue):

A further reason is that [Cause 2: long work hours/stressful commute]. As a result, people do not have enough energy to [exercise] when they get home.

Body 3 (Solutions - Schools & Funding):

It will not be easy to find a solution to this problem. I think [Entity 1: schools] have a key role to play by [Action]. Funding can also help provide a solution: the government should [Action 2].

Conclusion:

Lack of [activity] is a serious problem for [health], but the solutions suggested above could do a great deal to help solve it.
