Task IELTS Writing Task 1 Prompt

The world's media consumption

The graphs below give information about how many hours people spent every day using different media and how many hours they spent using the media in different parts of the world from 2012 to 2015.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

Hours spent using media per day, average, by source

Key Milestone: Online media grew from ~2.5 hrs to ~3.3 hrs, overtaking TV in 2014.

Hours spent using media per day, average, by region

Regional Gap: US media consumption (11–12h) was more than 4 hours higher than Asia (<8h).

Model answer & Method Annotations

178 words • Band 7.5+ Sample calibrated for Band 5 mastery
Introduction Paraphrase

Start by saying what the graphs show, but paraphrase the writing task.

💡 Replaces: "how many hours people spent every day using different media" with "data about the use of different media in different areas of the world".
Approximation Language

Use approximately or about when you are not sure of exact figures.

⭐ "from approximately 2.5 hours per day to about 3.3 hours per day."
Comparative & Superlative

Use comparative and superlative forms. Use figures from the graphs to illustrate the points you make.

💡 "spent the most hours per day... spent the least time... averaging over four hours per day less than US citizens."
Paragraph 1: Introduction (Dual Graph Paraphrase)

The charts provide data about the use of different media in different areas of the world over the period 2012-2015. They compare online use with use of TV, radio and print media over the period. They also show the average hours spent per day on media use in the USA, Latin America, Europe and Asia.

Paragraph 2: Graph 1 Features (Online Surge & Traditional Media Decline)

We can see from the data that online consumption increased over the period from approximately 2.5 hours per day to about 3.3 hours per day. This meant that in 2014, going online overtook TV as the type of media people gave most time to. TV, radio and print all showed a very slight decline between 2012 and 2015 in terms of hours spent per day on them.

Paragraph 3: Graph 2 Features (Superlatives, Regional Peaks & Differences)

Of the four geographical areas illustrated in the charts, people in the USA spent the most hours per day using media, rising from 11 to 12 hours per day over the period. People in Asia spent the least time on media, averaging over four hours per day less than US citizens.

Referencing & Cohesion

Use words like this and that to refer back to ideas.

💡 "This meant that in 2014, going online overtook TV..."
Paragraph Structure

You can write a separate paragraph about each of the graphs.

⭐ Para 2 handles Media Source trends; Para 3 handles Regional differences.
Time & Units Precision

Always mention the exact units: hours per day and years (2012–2015).
