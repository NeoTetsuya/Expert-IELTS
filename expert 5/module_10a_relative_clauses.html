Module 10 Grammar Guide

Defining Relative Clauses

Learn how to securely link ideas together and specify exactly who or what you are talking about using relative pronouns.

What is their purpose?

We use defining relative clauses to give essential information about a person, thing, or place. Without this clause, the sentence would be too general or wouldn't make complete sense.

"The man who lives next door is very friendly."

Why it's needed: If we just say "The man is very friendly", you wouldn't know which man. The clause defines him exactly.

"Dogs that like cats are very unusual."

Why it's needed: We aren't talking about all dogs in the world, only the specific subset "that like cats". Removing this clause changes the meaning of the sentence entirely.

Hover over the cards below to see how different pronouns connect to different subjects:

who or that

For People

"There are very few people who write letters these days."

"The woman that called you is my boss."

which or that

For Things & Animals

"I’d like to get a phone which has a good camera."

"The umbrella that I bought is broken."

whose

To Show Possession

Replaces his, her, its, their.

"I think I know whose telephone number this is."

"The man whose wallet I found was grateful."

where

For Places

Used specifically for physical locations.

"There’s a café next door where you can get wi-fi."

"This is the park where we first met."

The Golden "That" Rule

The pronoun that can replace who or which, but it cannot ever replace whose or where.

A relative pronoun acts as either the subject or the object of the clause. This completely changes the rules of omission!

1. As SUBJECT

Cannot Omit ⛔

It performs the action.

Trick: Followed directly by a VERB.

"There are apps which are free."

'which' is the subject of the verb 'are'.

"The woman who lives next door..."

'who' is the subject of the verb 'lives'.

✨

2. As OBJECT

Can be Omitted! ✅

It receives the action.

Trick: Followed by a NOUN/PRONOUN.

"There’s an app which I want."

"The book that she bought is useful."
