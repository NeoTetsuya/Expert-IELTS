Module 3 Grammar Guide

Present Continuous & Stative Verbs

Master ongoing actions! Learn how to describe what is happening right now, temporary situations, spelling changes with -ing, and why stative verbs stand apart.

When to Use the Present Continuous

Use be + -ing for actions occurring right now or for temporary states.

1

Actions Happening Now

Taking place at the exact moment of speaking.

"I'm trying to finish this report."

"You're not listening to me!"

2

Temporary Situations

Happening around now, but for a limited time period (e.g., this week, at the moment).

"I'm working in a different office this week."

"My computer isn't working at the moment."

How to Form It: be (am/is/are) + -ing

Positive (+)

  • I'm waiting
  • He / She / It 's waiting
  • You / We / They 're waiting

Negative (-)

  • I'm not waiting
  • He / She / It isn't waiting
  • You / We / They aren't waiting

Questions (?)

  • Am I waiting?
  • Is he / she / it waiting?
  • Are you / we / they waiting?

Spelling Rules for Verbs + -ing

Pay close attention to word endings before adding -ing.

Rule 1: Most Verbs

Add -ing

Simply append -ing to the base form.

start → starting

work → working

Rule 2: Ends in -e

Drop -e + -ing

Remove silent final -e before adding -ing.

live → living

take → taking

Rule 3: Ends in -ee

Keep -ee + -ing

Do NOT drop -e if double -ee.

agree → agreeing

see → seeing

Rule 4: Ends in -ie

Change -ie to -y

Replace -ie with -y, then add -ing.

lie → lying

die → dying

Rule 5: 1 Vowel + 1 Consonant

Double Consonant + -ing

For short single-syllable verbs ending in vowel + consonant, double the consonant.

sit → sitting

stop → stopping

Stative Verbs

Stative verbs describe states, feelings, or thoughts, NOT physical actions. They are not normally used in continuous forms.

💡

Important Rule

Say: "I need some help." (Present Simple)
Do NOT say: "I am needing some help."

Common Stative Verb Categories

🧠 1. Mental / Thinking
agree believe know remember think understand
❤️ 2. Attitude & Feelings
hate like love need prefer want wish
👁️ 3. Senses
hear see smell taste
✨ 4. Appearance & Qualities
appear look (=seem) seem sound
🔑 5. Possession & Owning
belong have own
🏷️ 6. Other States
be cost mean
