Prepositions: Place, Time & Movement

Master prepositions of place, time (at / in / on), and movement with clear examples and practice!

A. Prepositions of Place

Used to show where an object or person is located relative to something else.

Key Prepositions Bank (Page 80):

above among at below beside between in near next to on under
Example 1

under

Directly beneath or covered by something.

"There is a lot of rubbish under the bridge."
Example 2

next to

Directly beside or immediately at the side of.

"I live right next to the train station."
Example 3

near

Close distance (without necessarily touching).

"The bus stop is very near the college."

B. Prepositions of Time

Main prepositions: at, in, and on.

Specific Point / Time 🎯

AT

Clock times, specific time periods, meals, festivals, and age.

  • ⏱️ Clock: at 12 o'clock
  • 🌙 Periods: at midnight, at night, at the weekend
  • 🍳 Meals: at breakfast
  • 🎉 Festivals: at Diwali
  • 🎂 Age: at the age of 25
Long Periods / Parts ⏳

IN

Parts of the day, months, seasons, years, and centuries.

  • 🌅 Parts of day: in the morning
  • 📅 Months: in March
  • ❄️ Seasons: in winter
  • 📆 Years: in 2015
  • 🏛️ Centuries: in the 19th century
Days & Specific Dates 🗓️

ON

Specific days, dates, and special day celebrations.

  • 📅 Days: on Monday, on weekdays
  • 🎈 Special Days: on my birthday
  • 🎆 Holidays (day): on New Year's Day
  • 📌 Dates: on 13 April

⚡ Other Common Time Prepositions

before, after, by, since, for, during, until.

until = up to the point of time
"I can't afford to go on holiday until I start work."
during = throughout a period
"I went skiing during the winter holidays."

C. Prepositions of Movement

Used to show movement in the direction of or away from a place.

Prepositions of Movement List:

across along down into off onto out of over through to towards up
🏛️
through

Entering one side and exiting the other side of an enclosed space.

"I like walking through the old part of the city."

⛰️
up / down

Ascending to a higher point / Descending to a lower point.

"We ran up the hill."
