Module 6 Grammar Guide

Future Forms: will & be going to

Predicting the future and making plans! Learn exactly when to use "will" and when to use "be going to" to express your future intentions.

Be Going To

Used for things we have already thought about or can clearly see.

1 Intentions & Plans

You have already decided to do this action before speaking.

"We're going to join a gym next week."

2 Predictions based on Evidence

Saying what we expect to happen based on what we know or see now.

"This meal is going to be amazing – I hear the restaurant is very good."

How to Form It

Positive (+)

  • I'm going to stay.
  • He/She/It is going to stay.
  • You/We/They are going to stay.

Negative (-)

  • I'm not going to stay.
  • He/She/It isn't going to stay.
  • You/We/They aren't going to stay.

Questions (?)

  • Am I going to stay?
  • Is he/she/it going to stay?
  • Are you/we/they going to stay?

Will

Used for spontaneous ideas, offers, and general opinions.

1 Predictions

Often used with phrases like I think, I'm sure, probably, definitely.

"I think this recipe will be quite difficult."

2 Spontaneous Decisions

Decisions we make at the exact moment of speaking.

"I'm not hungry, so I'll just have a coffee, please."

3 Offers

Volunteering to do something for someone else.

"I'll help you make dinner."

Grammar Showdown

Toggle the switch to see how changing the phrase completely changes the context.

Scenario 1: Making a Prediction

"I think it'll be better to shop online."

Personal Opinion

Just a general thought or guess without any specific physical proof in front of you.

"I don't think those biscuits are going to taste very good – they look burnt."

Clear Evidence

You can physically see that they are burnt right now, so you know the result.

Scenario 2: Future Actions

"I’m not sure what to have – I think I'll have pizza."

Spontaneous

You just made this decision at this exact moment (perhaps looking at a menu).

"We're going to meet next weekend and go for a meal."

Pre-planned

You already organized this plan with your friends before this conversation started.
