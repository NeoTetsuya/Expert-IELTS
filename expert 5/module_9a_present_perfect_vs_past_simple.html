Module 9 Grammar Guide

Present Perfect vs. Past Simple

Master the difference between finished and unfinished time. Learn exactly when to use these two essential tenses to talk about the past.

The Present Perfect Connection

Formed with: have/has + past participle

We use the Present Perfect when there is a strong connection to the present. It is all about unfinished time or information.

1

Indefinite Past Time

We don't say exactly when the action happened. The experience itself is what matters.

"I have been to Spain."

2

Unfinished Action

The action started in the past and is still continuing right now.

"My brother has worked here since 2015."

3

Unfinished Time Period

The time period we are talking about (today, this week, this year) hasn't ended yet.

"I have been to the library 4 times this week."

The Past Simple Anchor

Formed with: Verb + ed (or irregular past form)

We use the Past Simple when the action is completely disconnected from the present. It is all about finished, completed events.

1

Specific Past Time

We explicitly state or know when the past action happened.

"I was in Spain last weekend."

2

Completed Action

The action started and definitively finished in the past. It's over.

"My brother worked here for five years."

3

Finished Time Period

The time period we are talking about (yesterday, last week) has completely ended.

"I went to the library 4 times last week."

Grammar Showdown

Toggle the switch to see how changing the tense completely changes the reality of the sentence.

Scenario 1: Travel

"I was in Spain last weekend."

Specific Time Mentioned

We are interested in exactly when you went. The time (last weekend) is finished.

"I have been to Spain."

Indefinite Time

We don't know or care exactly when. We are only interested in your life experience.

Scenario 2: Employment

"My brother worked here for 5 years."

Action Finished

He no longer works here. The 5-year period is completely in the past.

"My brother has worked here since 2015."

Action Continuing

He started in 2015 and he still works here right now.
