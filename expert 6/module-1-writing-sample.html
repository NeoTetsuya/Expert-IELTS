Task IELTS Writing Task 1 Prompt

The table below shows the wages of fast food workers between 2010 and 2012.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

Percentage of fast food workers on different pay rates 2010–2012

Pay rate / Hourly wage Age 16–19 (%) Age 20+ (%)
$7.25 or less (Minimum wage) 13.2 11.1
$7.26 – $10.09 (Majority rate) 70.4 68.0
$10.10 – $11.99 8.7 10.5
$12.00 or more (Highest tier) 7.8 10.4
💡
Dominant Wage Bracket: Around 70% of both age brackets earned between $7.26 and $10.09/hour.
🔍
Overall Age Disparity: Under 20s had higher representation at the lowest rate, while over 20s were more represented above $10/hour.

Model answer & Method Annotations

168 words • Model Answer calibrated for Band 6 Target
Introduction Paraphrase

Avoid using words from the original sentence in your introduction.

💡 Replaces: "wages of fast food workers" with "earnings of people employed in the fast food industry in the United States".
Idea Grouping

Avoid 'mechanical repetition' by organising ideas into main points.

⭐ Groups data into general sub-$10 workers, middle majority rates, and high-income extremes.
Data & Figures Precision

Use specific numbers from the chart to illustrate your points.

💡 Quotes: "13.2% and 11.1% respectively" and "70.4 percent of under 20s and 68 percent of over 20s".
Paragraph 1: Introduction & General Trend Overview

The table shows the earnings of people employed in the fast food industry in the United States. We can see from the data that, in general, people under the age of 20 earn less than those over the age of 20.

Paragraph 2: Main Body Features (Dominant Brackets & Group Comparison)

The majority of people working in these restaurants in both age groups earn below $10 an hour. In both age groups, $7.26-$10.09 was the most common rate of pay. However, the numbers for those earning the lowest wage was slightly higher for the 16-19 age group than the 20+ age group, at 13.2% and 11.1% respectively. However, the average for both groups is somewhere around $7.26 and $10.09, with 70.4 percent of under 20s and 68 percent of over 20s in this group. Only a very small number of people in both age groups were able to earn over $12.00 an hour.

Paragraph 3: Final Summary Sentence

So, wages for teenagers in the fast food industry were generally lower than for older workers.

Overview Requirement

Write an overview sentence, summarising the results.

Key Comparison Check

Verify your comparisons: check that you compared both the majorities (around 70%) and the extremes ($7.25 vs. $12.00+).
