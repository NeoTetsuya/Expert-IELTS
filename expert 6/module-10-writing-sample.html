Model answer & Method Annotations

178 words • Band 6 Model Answer
Paraphrase Prompt

Start by introducing the topic of the diagram but paraphrase the test task.

💡 "The two maps show how the city library in Westerby has changed since 1984."
Comparative Synthesis

Describe the maps in more detail, comparing features of both maps rather than speaking about one map and then the other.

⭐ "If we look more closely at the map, we can see that there were separate shelves... but today they are located together in the extension."
Paragraph 1: Introduction & Overall Structural Modernisation Overview

The two maps show how the city library in Westerby has changed since 1984. Overall the maps tell us that the library has been extended to the west. It has also been modernised with technology being an important feature.

Paragraph 2: Body 1 (Books Consolidation, Media Center & Children's Area)

If we look more closely at the map, we can see that there were separate shelves for fiction and non-fiction books in the south and east of the library in 1984 but today they are located together in the extension. The non-fiction bookshelves to the south have been replaced by the multi-media centre and the fiction bookshelves to the east are now full of DVDs and CDs. The children's book area still exists today but, like the adult books, it has also been moved to the extension.

Paragraph 3: Body 2 (Service Desks, Central Study Tables & Unchanged Room)

The librarian's desk that existed in 1984 does not exist today. Instead, visitors get help from an information desk to the north east of the library. The study area remains in the centre of the main room but is laid out in a different way. In fact, only the community meeting room is in the same location on both maps.

Overall Map Summary

Give an overview of the diagram. What is the main thing you learn?

💡 "Overall the maps tell us that the library has been extended to the west. It has also been modernised with technology being an important feature."
Verbs & Passive Voice

Use a range of verbs in the correct tense (check the dates on the map).

Use the passive voice as it is unlikely you know who changed each item: "has been extended", "have been replaced by", "has also been moved to".

Language of Location

Use language of location to describe where things are on the map: "to the west", "in the south and east", "in the extension", "to the north east", "in the centre of the main room".

Band 6 Map Toolkit (Module 10)

1. Comparative Map Report Skeleton

Introduction & Core Overview:

The two maps show how [facility/area] in [town] has changed since [year]. Overall, the maps tell us that [facility] has been [extended/expanded in direction], while also being [modernised/digitised with key new features].

Body 1 (Major Relocations & Replacements):

If we look more closely at the map, we can see that there were [original facilities] in the [compass directions] in [past year], but today they are [relocated/located together in new wing]. The [original feature] to the [direction] has been replaced by [new feature], while [other area] is now full of [new purpose]. [Item B] still exists, but has also been moved to [location].

Body 2 (Service Changes, Layouts & Unchanged Elements):

The [past facility] that existed in [past year] does not exist today; instead, visitors [receive services from new desk] to the [direction]. The [central area] remains in the centre but is laid out in a different way. In fact, only [single feature] is in the same location on both maps.
