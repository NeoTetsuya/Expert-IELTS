Task 2: Opinion essay

Expert writing

• The statement of opinion: You must say if you agree or disagree with the statement of opinion and give reasons in your essay.

Task IELTS Writing Prompt

Write about the following topic:

"Some people believe that museums should be free to everyone and not charge an entry fee.

To what extent do you agree?"

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

Model answer & Method Annotations

262 words • Model Answer calibrated for Band 6 Target
Essay Planning Strategy

Plan your essay before you start writing. Generate some ideas and then select the best. Organise your ideas into a paragraph plan.

💡 Plan 4 clean paragraphs: Introduction with thesis → Reason 1 (Educational value) → Reason 2 (Social equality) → Conclusion with government funding condition.
Body Paragraphs & Reasons

Write two or three paragraphs which answer the essay task, i.e. reasons why you agree or disagree with the opinion.

Use phrases to introduce reasons and examples. Include a conclusion that restates your opinion and reasons.

⭐ Para 2 starts with "One reason is that..." • Para 3 starts with "Another reason is that...".
Paragraph 1: Introduction (Context, Paraphrase & Clear Stance)

Museums offer us the opportunity to learn about a variety of subject areas in more depth, such as history or nature. While it is possible to go to some museums for free, the majority of them require a visitor\'s fee. This essay will argue that all museums should be free to everyone.

Paragraph 2: Body 1 (Reason 1: Public Education & Cultural Heritage)

One reason is that museums are an excellent source of education. They help us to understand our culture, and other people\'s culture, better by displaying objects from our past. For example, at the British Museum you can see objects from around the world which help us to understand world history. When museums charge people to enter, not everyone is able to pay and so fewer people have the opportunity to learn about that history.

Paragraph 3: Body 2 (Reason 2: Economic Fairness & Educational Opportunity)

Another reason is that when a museum charges, it only really affects people on lower incomes in society. Wealthier people will be able to afford to visit and as a result will become more educated. However, people on lower incomes will be less likely to afford a visit and will therefore not benefit from the museum in the same way. The effect of this is that not everyone has the same educational opportunities as others in our society.

Paragraph 4: Conclusion (Restatement & Realistic Funding Condition)

In conclusion, I would suggest that free entry to museums allows everyone to learn about history and culture. However, for this to be possible, the government will have to provide funding for the museums. Without money from visitors or government funds, museums will not be able to find, buy, take care of or display historical objects.

Introduction & Stance

Start with an introduction. Paraphrase the question and state your opinion.

💡 Replaces: "should be free to everyone and not charge an entry fee" with "While it is possible to go to some museums for free, the majority of them require a visitor's fee."
Grammar & Complex Sentences

Use relative clauses where appropriate to make more complex sentences.

Use the language of opinion (e.g. "This essay will argue that...", "I would suggest that...").

⭐ "objects from around the world which help us to understand world history."
Review & Edit Checklist

Re-read your essay. Check the ideas are organised well. Then, check your use of language and spelling.

✏️ Ensure your conclusion directly answers the prompt and restates your reasons.

Band 6 Opinion Essay Toolkit (Module 2)

1. Band 6 Opinion Essay Skeleton (Single-Stance Agreement)

Introduction:

[Topic] offers us the opportunity to [broad benefit]. While it is possible to [current status], the majority [situation]. This essay will argue that [clear opinion statement].

Body 1 (Reason 1 - Educational Access):

One reason is that [Point 1: excellent source of education]. They help us to understand [culture] by displaying [objects]. For example, at [specific institution] you can see [items] which help us understand [history]. When museums charge, not everyone is able to pay and so fewer people learn.

Body 2 (Reason 2 - Social Equality):

Another reason is that when [policy exists], it only really affects people on lower incomes. Wealthier people will afford it and become more educated. However, people on lower incomes will be less likely to afford it. The effect of this is unequal educational opportunities.

Conclusion & Practical Condition:

In conclusion, I would suggest that [restate opinion and benefits]. However, for this to be possible, the government will have to provide funding. Without funds, [consequence on preservation].
