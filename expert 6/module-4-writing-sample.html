Expert writing

• Opinion 1 & Opinion 2: You must give reasons for both statements and then give your opinion.

Task IELTS Writing Prompt

Write about the following topic:

"Technology has changed the way people communicate today.

[Opinion 1] Some people believe this is making our relationships stronger while [Opinion 2] others believe it is harming them.

Discuss both points of view and give your own opinion."

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

Model answer & Method Annotations

261 words • Model Answer calibrated for Band 6 Target
Introduction Paraphrase

Do NOT copy the information in the test task. Paraphrase it.

💡 Replaces prompt words with "developed at a very fast rate", "communicate regularly using smart technology and social networking sites".
Topic Sentence

Start paragraphs with a topic sentence to introduce the main point.

⭐ "Technology allows us to maintain closer connections to our friends and family."
Opposing Viewpoint

Start a new paragraph to introduce the opposing viewpoint.

💡 "On the other hand, technology can also stop us from giving our full attention to the people we are with."
Conclusion Strategy

Give your opinion in the conclusion, summarising the reasons.

⭐ "In my opinion, technology offers us fantastic opportunities... However... I therefore believe..."
Paragraph 1: Introduction (Background, Two Views & Essay Scope)

Technology has developed at a very fast rate in recent years. We are now able to communicate regularly with our friends and family using smart technology and social networking sites. While some people feel this can strengthen our relationships, others believe it has a more negative impact. This essay will discuss both these opinions.

Paragraph 2: Body 1 (View 1: Maintaining Closer Connections)

Technology allows us to maintain closer connections to our friends and family. For instance, we can send each other messages, make each other laugh and be there for each other in difficult times, even if we cannot physically be there. We no longer need to lose touch with old school friends or family members if we move away. The use of social media enables us to continue to share our lives.

Paragraph 3: Body 2 (View 2: In-Person Distraction & Weaker Bonds)

On the other hand, technology can also stop us from giving our full attention to the people we are with. Many of us check our messages regularly so we do not miss out on a piece of news. The result is that we stop listening to our friends or family in the same room as us because we are reading something else. When we ignore those people, our connections with them might become weaker and our relationships could suffer.

Paragraph 4: Conclusion (Writer's Opinion, Balanced Synthesis & Recommendation)

In my opinion, technology offers us fantastic opportunities for building relationships with the people we love because we maintain our connections. However, some people use it too much which means they do not spend enough time building face-to-face relationships. I therefore believe we should put our technology away when we are talking to our friends or family.

Essay Scope & Outline

State what the essay will say. Make sure it answers the test task.

💡 "This essay will discuss both these opinions." — Clear, direct roadmap for a 'Discuss Both Views' task.
Detail & Relevance

Explain the main point in more detail and give examples where possible. Make sure all information is relevant.

⭐ "For instance, we can send each other messages, make each other laugh and be there for each other in difficult times..."
Cohesion & Connectors

Use connectors to link ideas smoothly throughout your paragraphs.

⭐ "While...", "For instance", "On the other hand", "The result is that", "However", "I therefore believe".

Band 6 Discussion Formula

On the other hand, technology can also stop us from...

Why this achieves Band 6:

Presents a clear shift to the second required perspective in a Discussion essay, demonstrating paragraph-level cohesion and varied transition markers.

Band 6 Standard Pattern Example:

"While some people feel this can strengthen our relationships, others believe it has a more negative impact."

Band 6 Discussion Toolkit (Module 4)

1. Band 6 Discussion & Opinion Essay Skeleton

Introduction:

[Topic background]. We are now able to [paraphrase activity]. While some people feel [View 1], others believe [View 2]. This essay will discuss both these opinions.

Body 1 (View 1 - Positive / Strengthen):

[Topic sentence: Technology allows closer connections]. For instance, [examples: messages, laughter, emotional support]. We no longer need to lose touch when we move away. The use of [technology] enables us to share our lives.

Body 2 (View 2 - Negative / Harm):

On the other hand, [Topic sentence: stops us from giving full attention in person]. Many check messages so they do not miss out. The result is that we stop listening to people in the room. When we ignore them, connections become weaker.

Conclusion & Personal Stance:

In my opinion, [restate positive potential]. However, [acknowledge overuse drawback]. I therefore believe [practical balanced recommendation].
