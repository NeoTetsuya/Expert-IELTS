Task 2: Compare and contrast essay

Expert writing

• Compare and contrast the use of driverless cars with the use of cars today.

• Say if driverless cars will be positive or negative.

Task IELTS Writing Prompt

Write about the following topic:

"Soon it is likely we will use driverless cars to move around.

[Question 1] How similar or different will this be to how we travel today? [Question 2] Do you think it will be a positive or negative development?"

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

Topic Sentences

Start each paragraph with a topic sentence to state the main idea clearly.

💡 "Travelling in a driverless car will be very different from travelling in one controlled by a driver."

Each paragraph deals with one question in the test task.

⭐ "To sum up, driverless cars will be more interconnected than cars today... This should result in..."
Paragraph 1: Introduction (Background Context & Dual Thesis Roadmap)

Currently several companies are trialling driverless cars on the roads. These include technology companies and car manufacturers. As a result, people will soon travel around in driverless cars. This essay will argue that future car travel will be significantly different from now but that this change will be largely positive.

Paragraph 2: Body 1 (Question 1: Differences — Input & Satellite Interconnection)

Travelling in a driverless car will be very different from travelling in one controlled by a driver. Firstly, the motorist will simply say or type in a destination and the car will travel there. Secondly, the car will be connected to satellite technology. While this is true of some cars today, the majority are not connected in this way. The satellite technology will ensure that vehicles and traffic signals all speak to each other.

Paragraph 3: Body 2 (Question 2: Positive Development — Productivity & Accident Reduction)

The changes described should be a positive development. One reason is that drivers will no longer have to pay attention at all times. Instead, they will be able to do work, check emails or make phone calls safely. This will mean a more productive and less boring journey. In addition, there will be fewer accidents because safety will be controlled by the car and will not rely on the skills of the driver. The car will be able to make faster decisions to avoid hitting other vehicles or objects.

Paragraph 4: Conclusion (Key Point Synthesis & Prudent Safety Verification)

To sum up, driverless cars will be more interconnected than cars today. This should result in a safer and more relaxed journey for the driver. However, this technology is still in its early days and we do not yet know exactly how successful it will be. It is important that manufacturers fully test the technology to ensure the development is a positive one.

Dual Thesis Roadmap

Outline both answers in your thesis. State clearly that travel will be significantly different AND largely positive.

💡 "This essay will argue that future car travel will be significantly different from now but that this change will be largely positive."
Addition Connectors

Use addition connectors to build strong arguments (e.g. "In addition...").

⭐ Connects convenience (doing work/making calls) to road safety (avoiding human error).
Balanced Outlook

Add a realistic concluding condition acknowledging that emerging technology requires comprehensive safety testing.

✏️ "However, this technology is still in its early days... It is important that manufacturers fully test..."

1. Two-Part Compare & Contrast Essay Skeleton

Introduction:

Currently [companies/governments] are trialling [new technology]. As a result, [future change will occur]. This essay will argue that [future state] will be significantly different from now but that this change will be largely positive.

Body 1 (Question 1 - Similarities & Differences):

[Topic sentence: Travelling in X will be very different from Y]. Firstly, [Difference 1: automated input instead of physical steering]. Secondly, [Difference 2: satellite connectivity]. While this is true of some [current items], the majority are not connected in this way.

Body 2 (Question 2 - Positive Evaluation):

[Topic sentence: The changes described should be a positive development]. One reason is that [Benefit 1: drivers no longer need to pay constant attention, enabling safe work/calls]. In addition, [Benefit 2: fewer accidents because safety is managed computationally].

Conclusion & Caveat:

To sum up, [restate core differences and benefits]. However, [acknowledge early testing stage]. It is important that [stakeholders] fully test the technology to ensure the development is a positive one.
