Expert writing

• The trend: This is what you must write about.

• Identify the causes of international media dominance and its effects on national identity.

Task IELTS Writing Prompt

⏱️ Spend about 40 minutes on this task

Write about the following topic:

"An increasing number of people are choosing to watch international television shows and films. Less nationally produced entertainment is being made each year.

[Part 1: Causes] What do you think is causing this trend and [Part 2: Effects] what are the potential effects for national identity?"

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

Model answer & Method Annotations

262 words • Model Answer calibrated for Band 6 Target
Part 1: Causes Focus

Address the first part of the question (what is causing the trend) in one paragraph.

💡 "The main reason why national television companies buy more and more shows internationally is cost."
Cause & Effect Language

Use the language of cause and effect throughout your response.

⭐ "This leads to even more international content...", "because once the TV programmes have been paid for...", "Because of high sales..."
Part 2: Effects Focus

Address the second part of the question (potential effects on national identity) in a new paragraph.

💡 "The impact of this is mixed... there is a need for locally produced talent and variety..."
Paragraph 1: Introduction (Background Context & Explicit Thesis Statement)

In the early days of television, most of the programmes that appeared on our screens were made locally. Since the arrival of cable television and the internet, there has been more choice, and internationally successful shows, such as The Big Bang Theory, are enjoyed all around the world. This essay will argue that this is because of financial reasons, and suggests that we should be concerned.

Paragraph 2: Body 1 (Causes: Economics, Blockbuster Bundling & Production Costs)

The main reason why national television companies buy more and more shows internationally is cost. American TV companies often insist on selling their products in packages. When a local company wants to buy the current blockbuster, they often have to buy several poorer quality shows with it. This leads to even more international content on our screens, because once the TV programmes have been paid for, it makes sense to show them. It is also much cheaper to buy ready-made television shows than to produce them.

Paragraph 3: Body 2 (Effects: Production Quality vs. Cultural Identity & Subsidies)

The impact of this is mixed. Because of high sales, a lot of American television shows have large budgets, and can afford top-quality writers, actors and special effects. However, there is a need for locally produced talent and variety, and only local television companies can produce that. Some countries have started to support their local film and television industries, and it is not uncommon now to see specialised products, such as Scandinavian thrillers, or beautiful Iranian films, in other countries.

Paragraph 4: Conclusion (Globalisation Synthesis & National Identity Preservation)

In conclusion, the internationalisation of television and film is just part of a much larger process of globalisation. We need to find ways to build local and national talent within this new world order if we are going to enjoy all the talent around the globe.

Thesis Statement Placement

End your introduction with a thesis statement.

💡 "This essay will argue that this is because of financial reasons, and suggests that we should be concerned."
Elaboration & Logic

Make sure you give an explanation and reasons for your main points.

⭐ Explains how bundled TV packages and lower acquisition costs logically lead to saturated foreign programming.
Cultural Balance

Balance both sides of the effect: acknowledge high Hollywood production values while arguing for the vital necessity of local identity support.

Band 6 Cause & Effect Formula

This leads to even more international content...

Why this achieves Band 6:

Demonstrates clear causal progression and explanatory linking words ("leads to", "because once") rather than listing isolated, unsupported claims.

Band 6 Standard Pattern Example:

"The main reason why national television companies buy more and more shows internationally is cost."

Band 6 Cause & Effect Toolkit (Module 7)

1. Cause & Effect Essay Skeleton

Introduction & Thesis:

In the early days of [medium/industry], most [products] were made locally. Since the arrival of [technological change], there has been more choice, and [international items] are enjoyed worldwide. This essay will argue that this is because of [core cause], and suggests that we should [stance/concern].

Body 1 (Part 1: Causes & Explanations):

The main reason why [entities] [trend action] is [Cause 1: cost/convenience]. [Commercial factor / practice]. When [situation], they often have to [consequence]. This leads to even more [result], because once [condition], it makes sense to [action]. It is also much cheaper to [A] than to [B].

Body 2 (Part 2: Effects on National Identity & Culture):

The impact of this is [mixed/significant]. Because of [factor], [international producers] have large budgets and can afford [high quality elements]. However, there is a need for locally produced [talent and cultural identity], and only local companies can produce that. Some countries have started to [supportive policy].

Conclusion:

In conclusion, the [trend] is just part of a much larger process of globalisation. We need to find ways to build local and national [identity/talent] within this new world order if we are going to [overarching goal].
