Task IELTS Writing Prompt

⏱️ Spend about 40 minutes on this task

Write about the following topic:

"In many countries, the number of elderly people has increased significantly while the number of children has decreased.

[Part 1: Problems] What problems does this cause? [Part 2: Solutions] What solutions could help reduce these problems?"

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

Model answer & Method Annotations

268 words • Model Answer calibrated for Band 6 Target
Solution-Driven Topic Sentence

Start your first main body paragraph by suggesting a solution to the problem.

💡 "One issue is that people are choosing to have smaller families, and encouraging parents to have bigger families may help to change this."
Softening Recommendations

Use language to soften recommendations instead of making absolute claims.

⭐ "may help to change this", "Another potential solution may be to...", "can have a positive impact", "tends to be unpopular".
Conclusion & Recommendation

End with a conclusion that summarises the key points of the essay and makes a recommendation.

💡 "Change is needed both in terms of encouraging a higher birth rate and changing the nature of retirement, and no one solution can address the problems."
Paragraph 1: Introduction (Demographic Causes, Economic Impact & Thesis)

Improvements in standards of living and healthcare mean that people live longer, and families have fewer children these days. This has led to a rise in the average age of the population, which has created some economic problems. Older people often do not pay taxes, and may receive a pension from the government. This essay will suggest that multiple solutions are needed to reverse these trends.

Paragraph 2: Body 1 (Solution 1: Birth Subsidies & Evaluated Pros/Cons)

One issue is that people are choosing to have smaller families, and encouraging parents to have bigger families may help to change this. In some countries, the government offers money to help with the cost of raising a child. These children later grow up, work and pay tax. However, family size is a complex issue and depends on many factors. This solution has had limited success so far.

Paragraph 3: Body 2 (Solution 2: Raising Retirement Age & Experienced Labor)

Another potential solution may be to encourage older people to retire later. Sixty-year-olds today generally enjoy good health, and they can expect to live another twenty or thirty years in many countries. Delaying retirement by just five years can have a positive impact, as they will still be paying taxes, and often have a lot of expertise. However, this solution tends to be unpopular with people in their late fifties.

Paragraph 4: Conclusion (Fiscal Summary & Multi-Pronged Recommendation)

In conclusion, a higher number of older people in society results in higher spending and lower taxes for governments. Change is needed both in terms of encouraging a higher birth rate and changing the nature of retirement, and no one solution can address the problems. It is certainly an issue that all developed countries today are concerned about and it will affect us all.

Introductory Scope

Explain the causes and effects in the introduction before introducing the thesis roadmap.

💡 "Improvements in standards of living... mean that people live longer, and families have fewer children... This has led to a rise in the average age..."
Solution Evaluation

Show that you understand the advantages and disadvantages of the first solution.

⭐ Pro: children grow up and pay tax. Con: family size depends on complex factors and money grants have had limited success.
Demographic Precision

Use precise economic & demographic terms: birth rate, state pensions, standards of living, life expectancy, delay retirement.

Band 6 Problem-Solution Formula

Another potential solution may be to...

Why this achieves Band 6:

Demonstrates clear problem-solution progression, appropriate academic softening/hedging, and realistic policy evaluation without dogmatic claims.

Band 6 Standard Pattern Example:

"Another potential solution may be to encourage older people to retire later."

Band 6 Problem-Solution Toolkit (Module 9)

1. Demographic Problem-Solution Essay Skeleton

Introduction (Causes, Effects & Thesis):

Improvements in [living conditions/healthcare] mean that [people live longer], and [families have fewer children]. This has led to [demographic shift], which has created [macro-economic difficulties: pensions/tax base]. This essay will suggest that multiple solutions are needed to reverse these trends.

Body 1 (Solution 1: Birth Subsidies + Evaluation):

One issue is that [fertility rate decline], and encouraging [parents to have more children] may help to change this. In some countries, [governments provide financial grants/child benefits]. These children later [grow up and contribute taxes]. However, [family size is complex], and this solution has had [limited success].

Body 2 (Solution 2: Delayed Retirement + Softened Evaluation):

Another potential solution may be to [encourage later retirement]. Older citizens generally [enjoy better health] and can expect to [live longer]. Delaying retirement by [a few years] can have a positive impact as they [continue paying taxes and offer expertise]. However, this tends to be [unpopular with workers].

Conclusion:

In conclusion, [demographic aging] results in higher spending and lower tax revenues. Change is needed both in terms of [raising birth rates] and [reforming retirement], and no single measure can solve the issue.
