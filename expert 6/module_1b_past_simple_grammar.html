Past Simple Tense

Express completed past actions and general past states with confidence! Learn sentence structures and master essential irregular verbs.

2 Core Uses of the Past Simple

When to use the past tense of verbs.

1

Completed Past Actions

Actions that both started and finished at a specific time in the past.

"The man sent a text message."

"I finished work early."

2

General Past Situations

Past states, habits, or facts that were true over a period of time in the past.

"I studied Biology at university."

"Sending emails became popular in the 1990s."

Sentence Structures

Positive (+)

Subject + Verb + -ed (or Irregular)

"They filmed this show in New York."

"I went home early."

Negative (-)

Subject + didn't + Infinitive

"I didn't see the article."

💡 Note: Main verb stays in base form!

Question (?)

Did + Subject + Infinitive

"Did you finish the movie?"

✓ Yes, I did. / ✗ No, I didn't.
