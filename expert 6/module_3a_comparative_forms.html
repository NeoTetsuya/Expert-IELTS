A. Syllable-Based Rules for Adjectives & Adverbs

How to build comparatives (-er / more) and superlatives (-est / most).

1-Syllable ⚡

+er / +est endings

Add -er for comparatives and -est for superlatives.

  • Standard: fast → faster → fastest
    👉 "It's the fastest way to learn new words."
  • Vowel + Consonant (Double): big → bigger, sad → sadder
    👉 "The new school was bigger than the old one."
  • Ending in -e (+r / +st): safe → safer → safest
2-Syllables ⚖️

more / most OR -ier

Use more / the most for standard adjectives; replace -y with -ier / -iest.

  • Standard 2-syllables:
    👉 "The results were more interesting than imagined."
    👉 "Maths was the most popular subject."
  • Ending in -y (-ier / -iest):
    heavy → heavier → heaviest
3+ Syllables 🌟

more / the most

Always use more (comparative) and the most (superlative) for long adjectives and adverbs.

  • Long Adverb:
    👉 "He spoke more seriously than before."
  • Long Adjective:
    👉 "The Hadron Collider is the most important project."

B. Comparatives with Multiples (Page 45)

Formulas for expressing ratios: twice as...as, half as...as, and 3x+ multipliers.

1. Twice / Half 2️⃣

twice / half + as + adjective + as

With twice or half, we ALWAYS use the equative structure as + adjective + as.

👉 "Sport was twice as popular as art among primary school children."

👉 "The other class is only half as big as this one."

⚠️ CRITICAL TRAP: Do NOT use "more" or "-er" with twice/half!

❌ Incorrect: "The number of males was twice higher than females."

✅ Correct: "The number of males was twice as high as the number of females."

2. Three Times or More (3x+) 3️⃣

Dual Option Choice

With multipliers of three times or more, you can use EITHER structure:

  • Option A: three times + adjective + -er + than 👉 "Pass rates were four times higher than last year."
  • Option B: three times + as + adjective + as 👉 "Pass rates were four times as high as last year."
💡 Both options are 100% grammatically correct for 3x, 4x, 5x, etc.!

Page 45 Guardrail Review: Avoid These 3 Exam Errors!

  • ❌ Mistake 1: Using more with twice or half. (Say: twice as high as, NOT twice higher).
  • ❌ Mistake 2: Double comparatives (Say: more interesting, NOT more more interesting or more bigger).
  • ❌ Mistake 3: Forgetting to double the consonant in 1-syllable vowel + consonant words (e.g. bigger, sadder).

⚖️ Comparatives (-er / more)
  • Focus: Compares TWO items, groups, or periods directly.
  • Key Indicator: Always followed by than when comparing two things.
  • Example: "The new library is bigger than the old one."
🏆 Superlatives (-est / most)
  • Focus: Compares ONE item against THREE OR MORE items in a set/group.
  • Key Indicator: Preceded by the (e.g. the highest, the most famous).
  • Example: "It is the most important project in Europe."
