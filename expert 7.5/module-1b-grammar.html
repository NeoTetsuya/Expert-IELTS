Academic & IELTS Tense Rules

Specific tense protocols for IELTS Academic Writing Task 1, Task 2 essays, and Stative Verb exceptions.

IELTS Task 1 Data

Temporal Graphs & Charts

  • Past Dates (e.g. 1990–2010): Use Past Simple ("The percentage rose sharply in 2005").
  • Unspecified / Up to Present: Use Present Perfect ("Sales have increased since last year").
  • Future Projections: Use passive future forms ("is predicted to increase", "will have reached").
IELTS Task 2 Essays

Academic Argumentation

  • General Truths & Opinions: Use Present Simple ("Many educators argue that...").
  • Recent Scientific Developments: Use Present Perfect ("Recent studies have demonstrated...").
  • Hypothetical Consequences: Use second conditional ("If governments invested...").
Grammar Trap

Stative vs. Dynamic Verbs

Stative verbs (mental states, feelings, senses) normally cannot be used in Continuous tenses:

❌ I am knowing the answer.

✔️ I know the answer.

Stative List: know, believe, belong, understand, prefer, contain, exist.
