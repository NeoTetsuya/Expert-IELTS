Active Voice Structure

Subject + Active Verb + Object

Focus: Direct attention is on the Doer / Agent executing the action.

Passive Voice Structure

Receiver + Auxiliary BE + Past Participle (V3)

Focus: Direct attention is shifted to the Receiver / Result / Product.

💡 Passive Conversion Rule Breakdown:

Band 8.0+ Impersonal & Reporting Passives

Elevate informal opinion statements into formal academic claims using reporting passives and causative forms.

Pattern 1: Impersonal Dummy 'It'

It + is/was + Past Participle + that clause

Avoids subjective phrases like "I think" or informal generalizations like "People say".

❌ Low-Band / Informal:

"People believe that global warming affects weather."

✔️ Band 8.0+ Academic Form:

"It is widely believed that global warming affects weather patterns."

Key Reporting Verbs: argued, asserted, claimed, estimated, hypothesized, recognized, acknowledged.
Pattern 2: Subject Fronting

Subject + is/was + V3 + to + Infinitive

Places the central topic at the start of the sentence for academic prominence.

Present / General Claims:

"Renewable energy is considered to be the most sustainable solution."

Completed Historical Claims (to have + V3):

"Deforestation is estimated to have contributed to carbon levels."

Infinitive Choice: Use to + V1 for present state; use to have + V3 for past completions.

Additional Advanced Passive Structures

Causative Passive
Have / Get something done

Used when arranging for a third party or specialist to perform a task for you.

"The university had the laboratory equipment calibrated by technicians."

Prepositional Passive
Verbs with Mandatory Prepositions

When phrasal or prepositional verbs become passive, the preposition stays directly attached to the verb.

"The proposed proposal was objected to by community leaders."

Academic Process Protocols & Common Traps

Rules for IELTS Task 1 process diagrams and intransitive verb limitations.

IELTS Task 1 Standard

Describing Industrial & Natural Processes

Man-made processes (factory production, recycling) require Present Simple Passive because humans perform the steps:

  • Step 1 (Raw Material): "Raw limestone is crushed into fine powder."
  • Step 2 (Heating/Treatment): "The mixture is heated in a high-temperature kiln."
  • Step 3 (Packaging): "Finally, the cement is packaged into bags."
Grammar Trap

Intransitive Verbs CANNOT Be Passive

Intransitive verbs do not take a direct object. Therefore, they never have a passive form!

❌ The accident was happened yesterday.

✔️ The accident happened yesterday.

❌ The sun is risen in the east.

✔️ The sun rises in the east.

Intransitive Blacklist:

happen, occur, exist, arise, collapse, die, fall, rise, disappear, emerge.
