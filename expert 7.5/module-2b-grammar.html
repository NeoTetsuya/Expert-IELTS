Defining vs Non-Defining Clauses

Side-by-side comparative mechanics: essential identification vs. incidental extra information.

Essential Clause No Commas ❌

Defining Relative Clauses

Provides essential identification. Removing this clause alters the sentence core or leaves it ambiguous.

Subject Pronoun Example:

"This is the research report that was published yesterday."

Object Pronoun Omission (Contact Clause):

"The professor [whom] we interviewed was very knowledgeable."

✔️ 'That' Substitution: Fully permitted for both who and which.

✔️ Object Omission: Allowed when the pronoun acts as the object of the clause.

Non-Essential Detail Requires Commas ✔️

Non-Defining Relative Clauses

Adds extra, non-essential commentary. The antecedent is already fully identified; removing the clause leaves a complete, unambiguous sentence.

Extra Info Example:

"The new facility, which cost $5 million, will open next month."

Sentential 'Which' (Modifies Whole Clause):

"The policy was approved unanimously, which surprised everyone."

❌ 'That' Substitution: FORBIDDEN! Never replace which or who with that in non-defining clauses.

❌ Pronoun Omission: FORBIDDEN! Relative pronouns can NEVER be dropped.

💡 Reduction Mechanism Breakdown:

Band 8.0+ Preposition Fronting

Elevate informal "dangling prepositions" into formal academic fronted structures (*to which*, *in which*, *upon which*).

Degree & Extent

The degree / extent to which...

Replaces informal ending prepositions like "the extent that it works to".

❌ Informal / Dangling:

"This shows the degree that technology changes society to."

✔️ Band 8.0+ Fronted Form:

"This illustrates the degree to which technology transforms society."

Foundation & Basis

The basis / platform upon which...

Replaces informal endings like "the idea they built it on".

❌ Informal / Dangling:

"These are the principles that democratic systems rely on."

✔️ Band 8.0+ Fronted Form:

"These are the core principles upon which democratic systems rely."

Means & Instrument

The means by which...

Replaces informal phrases like "the way that they fix it by".

❌ Informal / Dangling:

"We need a method that we can measure progress by."

✔️ Band 8.0+ Fronted Form:

"We require a valid metric by which to measure progress."
