Epistemic Modality Knowledge & Belief 🧠

Logical Deduction & Likelihood

Reflects the speaker's judgment about the truth or probability of a proposition based on available evidence.

Deduction (Must):

"She must be in her office because the lights are on."

→ Speaker concludes it is logically 95% certain.

Speculation (May/Might):

"It might rain later, so bring an umbrella."

→ Speaker assesses a 30-50% chance.

Deontic Modality Obligation & Rules 📜

Duty, Permission & Social Rules

Reflects social obligations, prohibitions, official rules, permissions, or personal commitments to act.

Obligation (Must):

"You must submit the essay by Friday midnight."

→ Official deadline requirement.

Permission (May/Can):

"Students may leave the exam room after 60 minutes."

→ Official institutional permission granted.

Academic Hedging & Tone Softening Studio

Prevent Task Achievement score reductions in IELTS Task 2 by applying epistemic modals and hedging verbs.

Low-Band Trap

Categorical / Overgeneralized Claims

Stating conclusions as 100% absolute facts without scientific proof leads to examiner penalties for unhedged generalizations.

❌ Dogmatic Claim 1:

"Artificial intelligence will replace all human teachers by 2040."

❌ Dogmatic Claim 2:

"Urban migration causes crime rates to spike in major cities."

Band 8.0+ Precision

Scientifically Hedged Writing

Combining epistemic modals (*may, could*) with softening verbs (*suggest, tend to*) demonstrates academic restraint.

✔️ Hedged Form 1:

"It is conceivable that artificial intelligence could potentially automate certain instructional roles by 2040."

✔️ Hedged Form 2:

"Studies indicate that rapid urban migration tends to correlate with increased municipal challenges."

Academic Softening Vocabulary Toolkit

Epistemic Modals

may, might, could, can

"This policy could lead to improved outcomes."

Softening Verbs

suggest, indicate, appear to, tend to

"Data appears to indicate a gradual rise."

Probability Adverbs

presumably, arguably, conceivably, highly likely

"It is arguably the most viable alternative."
