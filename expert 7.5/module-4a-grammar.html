Academic Stance No Comma Needed ❌

Subordinating 'That' Clauses

A subordinating that clause cannot stand alone as a sentence. It acts as the object of reporting or stance verbs (*agree, argue, assert, claim, report, think*).

Expert Consensus Form:

"Many medical experts agree that excessive sugar consumption poses serious health risks."

Student Belief Form:

"Most candidates believed that the university entrance examination was fair."

Band 8.0+ Repair Comma Splice Trap ⚠️

Eliminating Comma Splices

Joining two independent clauses with only a comma creates a major grammatical error known as a comma splice.

❌ Comma Splice Error:

"Tuition fees are escalating rapidly, many students cannot afford university."

✔️ Repaired with Subordination:

"Because tuition fees are escalating rapidly, many students cannot afford university."

3 High-Band Fixes for Comma Splices

Method 1: Subordination

Add a subordinating conjunction (*because, although, since*).

"Because fees are rising, students struggle."

Method 2: Semicolon + Adverb

Use semicolon + transitional adverb (*consequently, therefore*).

"...escalating rapidly; consequently, students..."

Method 3: Participle Clause

Convert second clause to active present participle (*preventing*).

"...escalating rapidly, preventing many from..."

Adverbial Clauses & Complex Sentence Synthesis

Synthesize independent thoughts into logical complex sentences using adverbial clauses of Time, Cause, Contrast, Purpose, and Result.

Cause & Reason

Because / Since / As / Given that

Connects an outcome directly to its motivating cause or underlying reason.

Front Position (Comma Required):

"Given that public transport is heavily subsidized, urban commute times have decreased."

Middle Position (No Comma):

"Urban commute times have decreased since public transport is heavily subsidized."

Purpose & Result

So that / In order that / So... that

Expresses the intended goal (*so that*) or the degree of an outcome (*so... that* / *such... that*).

Purpose Clause (Target Goal):

"Governments tax sugary drinks so that citizens consume healthier alternatives."

Degree Result Clause (So + Adj + That):

"The inflation rate was so high that consumers reduced spending."
