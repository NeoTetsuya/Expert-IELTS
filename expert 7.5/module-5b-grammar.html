The Master Noun Phrase Formula
1. Determiner / Quantifier The / A majority of / These
2. Pre-Modifier(s) unprecedented financial
3. HEAD NOUN ★ DISPARITY
4. Post-Modifier(s) between urban and rural districts

→ Combined Output: "The unprecedented financial disparity between urban and rural districts..."

Pre-Modification Types

Modifying Before the Head Noun

Adjective Pre-modifier:

"Prominent positions are held by leaders."

Noun Modifier (Compound Noun):

"A database designer was hired."

Participle Pre-modifier (-ing / -ed):

"Groundbreaking research yielded unexpected results."

Post-Modification Types

Modifying After the Head Noun

Prepositional Phrase Post-modifier:

"The participants in the study answered questions."

Relative Clause Post-modifier:

"Houses which are located in low land face risks."

Appositive Noun Post-modifier:

"Dr. Aris, a leading climatologist, presented data."

Quantity & Statistical Data Noun Phrases

Master exact noun phrase formulas for reporting statistics, fractions, majorities, and survey trends in IELTS Task 1.

Fraction Formula A (fraction) + of + Category

Fractional Noun Phrases

Combines an indefinite article or determiner with a fractional noun phrase before a prepositional category.

20% Sample:

"A fifth of homeowners wanted to relocate."

75% Sample:

"Three-quarters of respondents supported the policy."

Proportion Formula The/A (majority/minority) + of + Category

Majority & Minority Noun Phrases

Modifies proportional nouns (*majority, minority, proportion, percentage*) with descriptive adjectives.

Dominant Share (>75%):

"The vast majority of young adults favor remote work."

Minimal Share (<10%):

"A negligible minority of participants opposed the change."

Academic Nominalization Studio

Transform wordy verbal clauses into compact, authoritative noun phrases to increase informational density.

Conversational Style

Verbal Clause Form (Low Band)

Relies heavily on active/passive verb phrases and adverbial conjunctions (*because, when, if*).

❌ Conversational Draft 1:

"Because technology developed rapidly, people communicated faster."

❌ Conversational Draft 2:

"If the government invests heavily in healthcare, public health will improve greatly."

Band 8.0+ Precision

Nominalized Noun Phrase (High Band)

Packages actions into abstract head nouns modified by pre/post-modifiers.

✔️ Band 8.0+ Nominalization 1:

"The rapid development of technology led to faster communication."

✔️ Band 8.0+ Nominalization 2:

"Heavy government investment in healthcare precipitates a significant improvement in public health."
