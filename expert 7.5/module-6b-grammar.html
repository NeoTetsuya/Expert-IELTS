It-Cleft Sentences & Element Spotlight

Learn how It + be + [emphasized item] + that/who... splits a sentence to highlight specific elements.

The Master It-Cleft Formula
1. Dummy Subject & Verb It + is / was
2. EMPHASIZED ELEMENT ★ IN FRANCE
3. Relative Pronoun that / who
4. Remaining Clause I learned how to sail

→ Combined Output: "It was in France that I learned how to sail."

Interactive It-Cleft Focus Spotlight

Dynamic Focus Shift

Standard Base Sentence: "Professor Aris published the climate report in Geneva last month."

Tense Agreement

Match 'be' with Main Verb

If the base sentence is past tense (*published*), use was. If present (*publishes*), use is.

Pronoun Selection

That vs. Who / Whom

That can replace places, things, time, and people. Who is used specifically when emphasizing human subjects.

Pronoun Case Rule

Subject / Object Pronouns

Formal academic style uses subject pronouns: "It was she who designed the protocol" (informal: "It was her...").

Wh- & Pseudo-Cleft Sentences

Package actions and concepts into What + S + V + is/was + [emphasized item] or reverse pseudo-cleft structures.

Standard Wh-Cleft End-Focus ★

What + Subject + Verb + is/was

Pushes the new, emphasized noun phrase or clause to the end of the sentence following is/was.

Standard Uncleft:

"I need to focus on my research proposal."

✔️ Wh-Cleft Form:

"What I need to focus on is my research proposal."

Reverse Pseudo-Cleft Front-Focus ★

[Emphasized Item] + is/was + what...

Places the key concept at the absolute front of the sentence for immediate dramatic emphasis.

Standard Uncleft:

"We require a comprehensive green energy policy."

✔️ Reverse Cleft Form:

"A comprehensive green energy policy is what we require."

Action Emphasis Pseudo-Clefts (Emphasizing Verbs)

To emphasize an entire verb action or event, use What + Subject + do/did + is/was + (to) + bare infinitive:

Past Action Emphasis

Base: "The committee approved the budget proposal."

"What the committee did was (to) approve the budget proposal."

All-Cleft (Exclusive Action)

Base: "I only requested a brief summary."

"All that I did was (to) request a brief summary."

Academic Thesis Clefts for IELTS Task 2

Elevate topic sentences and thesis statements using specialized rhetorical cleft frameworks.

Causal Thesis

The reason why... is that

Highlights the underlying cause of an issue before delivering the main explanation.

Sample Essay Sentence:

"The primary reason why renewable adoption lags is that initial infrastructure costs remain high."

Solution Thesis

It is only by / through... that

Establishes an indispensable prerequisite method for solving a problem.

Sample Essay Sentence:

"It is only through decisive legislative reform that wealth inequality can be curtailed."

Minimalist Thesis

All that is needed is...

Demonstrates that a complex problem requires a simple, single key action.

Sample Essay Sentence:

"All that is required to stimulate innovation is targeted grant funding for research universities."
