Error Type 1

Sentence Fragments

An incomplete thought punctuated as a full sentence. It lacks either a subject, a finite verb, or a main clause.

❌ "Because the experiment failed."

✔️ "Because the experiment failed, the team restarted."

Error Type 2

Run-On Sentences

Two independent main clauses merged together without any conjunction or punctuation mark separating them.

❌ "The lab closed early we went home."

✔️ "The lab closed early, so we went home."

Error Type 3

Comma Splices

Joining two independent clauses with only a comma. Commas cannot join two complete sentences alone!

❌ "Costs rose, demand plunged."

✔️ "Costs rose; consequently, demand plunged."
