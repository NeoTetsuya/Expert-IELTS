IELTS Task 1 Data Prepositions Masterclass

Using incorrect prepositions when reporting numerical trends completely changes factual meaning and reduces Task Achievement scores.

1. Difference / Margin

increased BY 15%

Indicates the net mathematical margin of change between the start and end values.

"Sales increased by 15% (from 50% to 65%)."
Formula: Verb + BY + Amount
2. Final Endpoint

rose TO 65%

Identifies the final destination figure reached after the trend completed.

"Sales rose to 65% in 2020."
Formula: Verb + TO + Final Number
3. Noun Margin

a surge OF 2,000

Follows trend nouns (*increase, surge, drop, decline, rise*) to express the numerical gap.

"There was a surge of 2,000 units."
Formula: Noun + OF + Number
4. Exact Static Point

peaked AT 92%

Pinpoints static landmarks or high/low points (*peaked, stood, plateaued, started*).

"Consumption peaked at 92%."
Formula: Static Verb + AT + Number

Academic Dependent Preposition Collocations

Certain verbs, adjectives, and nouns require specific dependent prepositions in formal academic prose.

Verbs + Preposition

Dependent Verb Patterns

  • attribute TO / ascribe TO:

    "Scholars attribute the surge to technological advancement."

  • correlate WITH:

    "Urban density correlates with public transport usage."

  • result IN (Cause) / result FROM (Effect):

    "Emissions result in warming."

Adjectives + Preposition

Dependent Adjective Patterns

  • susceptible TO / vulnerable TO:

    "Coastal zones are susceptible to sea-level rise."

  • compatible WITH / aligned WITH:

    "The policy is compatible with green targets."

  • inherent IN / intrinsic TO:

    "Financial risks are inherent in speculation."

Nouns + Preposition

Dependent Noun Patterns

  • impact ON / influence ON:

    "Tax reform has a direct impact on inflation."

  • insight INTO / inquiry INTO:

    "The trial offers key insight into cell behavior."

  • solution TO / remedy FOR:

    "Solar energy is a viable solution to power deficits."
