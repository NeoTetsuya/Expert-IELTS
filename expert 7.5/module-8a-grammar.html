Future Probability & Future in the Past

Probability Scale

Likelihood & Certainty Structures

Replace simple predictions (*will / might*) with precise probability adjectives followed by to-infinitives.

90–100% Inevitability (bound to / certain to):

"With glowing reviews, the movie is bound to be a major success."

70–80% Probability (likely to):

"Living costs in urban hubs are likely to escalate further."

10–20% Unlikelihood (unlikely to / doubtful whether):

"Unfunded research initiatives are unlikely to yield immediate breakthroughs."

Retrospective View

Future in the Past Mechanics

Expressing a future action or intention viewed from a past vantage point (frequently unfulfilled or interrupted).

Past Intention (was / were going to + V1):

"They were going to fly to Paris, but flight cancellations forced a delay."

Past Belief (would + V1):

"She assumed she would accept the corporate offer before changing her mind."

IELTS Task 1 Future Forecasting Toolkit

Elevate graph descriptions extending into future years (e.g. up to 2040 or 2050) using passive predictive formulas.

Formula 1

is / are projected to + V1

Reports statistical model forecasts with high academic neutrality.

Sample Graph Sentence:

"Solar power generation is projected to climb to 45% by 2040."

Formula 2

is / are set to / on track to

Highlights an ongoing trend moving steadily toward a expected future outcome.

Sample Graph Sentence:

"Electric vehicle adoption is set to surpass traditional engines by 2035."

Formula 3

By [Year] + Subject + will have V3

Establishes a completed benchmark quantity prior to a specific cutoff year.

Sample Graph Sentence:

"By 2050, the urban population will have doubled in comparison to 2010 metrics."
