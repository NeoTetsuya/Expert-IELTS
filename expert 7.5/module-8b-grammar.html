💡 Grammatical Rule Key:

Clause Connectors + SUBJECT + VERB ✔️

Subordinating Cause Clauses

Connects an outcome directly to a complete subordinating clause containing a subject and conjugated verb.

because / as / since:

"Students struggle because tuition fees are rising."

given that / seeing that (Academic):

"Given that renewable infrastructure requires capital, subsidies are vital."

Prepositional Connectors + NOUN / GERUND ✔️

Prepositional Cause Phrases

Requires a noun phrase, gerund (-ing), or nominalized noun phrase. CANNOT take a full finite verb directly unless followed by the fact that.

because of / due to / owing to:

"Students struggle because of escalating tuition fees."

due to the fact that (+ Clause Bridge):

"Students struggle due to the fact that tuition fees are escalating."

Cause → Effect Direction

Active Forward Causal Verbs

Subject (Cause) performs an action that triggers or produces the Object (Effect).

precipitate / give rise to / engender:

"Uncontrolled urban sprawl precipitates / gives rise to severe traffic gridlock."

trigger / result in / lead to:

"Heavy industrial emissions result in environmental degradation."

Effect ← Cause Direction

Reverse Origin Causal Verbs

Subject (Effect) originates from or is attributed to the Object (Cause).

stem from / originate in / result from:

"The current educational crisis stems from prolonged budget cuts."

be attributed to / be ascribed to:

"The decline in crime can be attributed to improved community policing."

IELTS Task 2 Causal Essay Formulas

Standardized high-band sentence blueprints for Cause & Solution or Cause & Effect essay types.

Blueprint 1

Causal Topic Sentence

Establishes the primary cause at the start of a body paragraph.

Sample Essay Formula:

"The primary factor responsible for [Issue] lies in [Cause Noun Phrase]."

Blueprint 2

Elaboration / Chain Effect

Explains how the cause leads step-by-step to subsequent consequences.

Sample Essay Formula:

"As a direct consequence of [Cause], [Effect Clause], thereby [Gerund Effect]."

Blueprint 3

Solution Remedy Link

Links targeted government intervention to reversing the negative cause.

Sample Essay Formula:

"By implementing [Solution], governments can effectively counteract [Negative Effect]."
