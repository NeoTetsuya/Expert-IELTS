💡 Technical Functional Verb Formulas:

Pattern 1: Purpose

is used to / is designed to + V1

Passive purpose formulation focusing on the intended engineering role of the component.

"The condenser is a device which is designed to convert steam back into liquid."

Pattern 2: Capability

allows / enables + Object + to + V1

Describes how a specific mechanism facilitates subsequent operational steps.

"The pressure valve allows excess gas to escape safely from the chamber."

Physical Appearance Vocabulary Toolkit

Categorized terminology for describing object Structure, Dimensions, Material Composition, and Geometric Form.

1. Structure

Component Assembly

  • consists of / comprises: "The unit comprises three main sections."
  • incorporates / features: "It incorporates a cooling coil."
  • is divided into: "The tank is divided into compartments."
💡 Rule: 'comprises' DOES NOT take 'of'!
2. Size & Scale

Physical Measurement

  • in diameter / height / width: "It measures 15 cm in diameter."
  • magnified / to scale: "Shown on a magnified scale."
  • elongated / reduced: "An elongated steel cylinder."
💡 Use prepositions 'in' before dimension nouns.
3. Material

Composition Terms

  • constructed from / made of: "Constructed from reinforced alloy."
  • formed by / constituted of: "Formed by layers of fiberglass."
💡 Use 'made of' for intact raw substances.
4. Form & Shape

Geometric Appearance

  • shaped like / resembles: "It resembles a hollow funnel."
  • cylindrical / spherical / conical: "A cylindrical storage vessel."
💡 Use adjective forms ending in '-ical' or '-shaped'.
