💡 Understanding the "Hypothetical Reality" Concept

Unreal conditionals and wish structures express the opposite of actual historical reality. Compare what was said against the true facts:

Hypothetical Statement (Unreal)

"If I had taken more time, I would have continued my education."

"If I hadn't studied so hard, I wouldn't have passed the test."

Real-World Fact (What Actually Happened)

❌ Fact: The person did not take enough time and did not continue education.

✅ Fact: The person studied hard and successfully passed the test!

Third Conditional Formula

If + Past Perfect, would have + V3

Describes hypothetical results of past events that did not happen (or did happen in negative forms).

"If I had taken time, I would have continued education."

"If I hadn’t studied hard, I wouldn’t have passed."

Wish / If Only Timeframes

Past Perfect vs. Past Simple

Use Past Perfect for past regrets; use Past Simple for present hypothetical desires.

Past Regret: "I wish / If only I had gone home earlier."

Present Desire: "I wish / If only I had more money."

Suppose & What If

Suppose / What if + Past

Used to invite someone to imagine a hypothetical present or past scenario.

"Suppose I ’d gone home earlier?" (Past)

"What if I had more money?" (Present)

In Case & It's Time

Precaution & Obligation

In case expresses precautions; It's time indicates something should happen now.

"Take an umbrella in case it rains."

"It’s time they changed enrolment procedures."

⭐ Band 8.5+ Advanced Mastery Mixed Conditionals (Time Bridging)
Writing Task 2 & Speaking Part 3

Standard conditionals stick to one timeframe. Mixed Conditionals bridge past causes with present consequences (or vice versa):

Type A: Past Cause → Present Result

If + Past Perfect, would + Base Verb

"If the municipal government had upgraded the water network in 2015, the city would not face shortages today."

Past inaction causes present problem.

Type B: Permanent Trait → Past Consequence

If + Past Simple, would have + V3

"If that corporation were more adaptable, it would not have gone bankrupt during the tech boom."

Ongoing characteristic affected past event.

should have + V3

"You should have seen that programme." (It was a good idea, but you didn't.)

Hypothetical Result
would have + V3

"I would have been happier if my team won." (I was not happy.)

Possible Result

"I might have been on time if the bus were quicker."

Unreal Possibility
could have + V3

"We could have gone if we had money." (It was not possible.)

⭐ Band 8.0+ Retrospective Analysis Past Modals of Deduction & Criticism
Speaking Part 3 & Task 2 Argumentation

Speculating on historical actions or critiquing past policy failures requires precise past modal distinctions:

must have + V3 (95% Certain)

"The sudden collapse must have been caused by structural design flaws."

High-confidence past logical deduction.

couldn't have + V3 (Impossible)

"The company could not have foreseen the unprecedented global disruption."

Logical impossibility in the past.

should have + V3 (Past Advice)

"Authorities should have implemented strict emission quotas earlier."

Unfulfilled obligation or critique.
