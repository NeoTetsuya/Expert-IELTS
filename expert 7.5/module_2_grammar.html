Primary Reasons to Use the Passive Voice

1
Changing Focus

Emphasizes the topic or result over the person responsible. Sentence-initial position gives primary prominence to the subject.

"The majority of rice is grown in Asia."

✔ Highlights topic/product over producer.
2
Unknown/Irrelevant Agent

Used when the doer is unknown, obvious from context, or unimportant to mention.

"This window has been left open."

"The patient was asked to fill out a form."

✔ Avoids unnecessary agent naming.
3
Academic Writing & IELTS

Standard in scientific reports and IELTS Task 1 process diagrams for objective step-by-step descriptions.

"A long pipe is connected to the turbine."

✔ Maintains objective formal academic tone.
⭐ Band 8.0+ Academic Passive Mastery Impersonal & Reporting Passive Structures
IELTS Writing Task 2 Essential

To maintain an objective, academic stance without overusing personal pronouns ("I think", "People believe"), high-band essays use two sophisticated impersonal passive constructions:

Pattern 1: "It is [thought/believed/argued] that..."

"It is widely acknowledged that early childhood education plays a pivotal role in cognitive development."

Common verbs: asserted, maintained, estimated, recognized, hypothesized.

Pattern 2: "[Subject] is [said/thought] to [have + V3]..."

"Deforestation is estimated to have contributed to over 15% of global greenhouse emissions."

Use "to + base verb" for present/general claims, or "to have + past participle" for completed historical actions.

⭐ Band 8.0+ Stylistic Precision Preposition Fronting in Relative Clauses
Writing Task 2 & Reading Analysis

Leaving prepositions dangling at the end of a clause ("the topic which we talked about") is common in informal speech, but placing the preposition before the relative pronoun ("the topic about which we spoke") produces an elevated academic register:

Degree / Extent

"The degree to which automation impacts employment remains disputed."

Replaces: "The degree that automation impacts employment to..."

Condition / Basis

"The fundamental pillars upon which democratic societies are established."

Replaces: "...pillars that societies are established on."

Method / Medium

"A mechanism by means of which heat energy is recycled."

Replaces: "...mechanism that energy is recycled by."
