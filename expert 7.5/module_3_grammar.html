Real Conditionals

Factual / Probable

Describe situations that are factually true, happen regularly, or are highly likely to happen.

Real Habit

"When I study, I listen to classical music."

Likely Future

"If I finish my work early, I go home."

Negative Real

"I won’t be able to move if I don’t save money."

✔ Expresses real habits, facts, and achievable conditions.

Unreal Conditionals

Hypothetical / Imaginary

Describe situations that are unlikely, impossible, or purely imaginary according to the speaker.

Unlikely Future

"If the government wanted to improve education, they would invest more."

→ Speaker considers this unlikely or impossible.

Imaginary Present

"If I understood economics, I’d be able to contribute."

→ Speaker does not understand economics currently.

✔ Expresses theoretical scenarios and imaginary states.

⭐ Band 8.0+ Advanced Syntax Grammatical Inversion in Conditional Clauses
Task 2 High-Scoring Structure

Omitting "if" and inverting the auxiliary verb with the subject creates formal, authoritative sentence structures that examiners prize for grammatical range:

Type 1 (Should + S + V)

"Should economic conditions deteriorate further, governments would need to intervene."

Replaces: "If economic conditions should deteriorate..."

Type 2 (Were + S + to-V)

"Were municipalities to invest in cycling infrastructure, congestion would drop markedly."

Replaces: "If municipalities were to invest..."

Type 3 (Had + S + V3)

"Had proactive policies been enacted earlier, the biodiversity loss could have been mitigated."

Replaces: "If proactive policies had been enacted..."

⭐ Band 8.0+ Academic Caution Hedging & Epistemic Modality in Argumentation
Writing Task 2 Band 9 Stance

Academic examiners penalize overgeneralizations (claiming something is 100% true without proof). Using modal hedging conveys appropriate scientific caution and balanced analysis:

❌ Overgeneralized (Low Band)

"Social media causes mental illness in adolescents."

✔ Appropriately Hedged (Band 8.0+)

"Compelling evidence suggests that compulsive social media use may potentially contribute to depressive symptoms among adolescents."

❌ Dogmatic Stance (Low Band)

"Renewable energy will completely replace fossil fuels by 2035."

✔ Appropriately Hedged (Band 8.0+)

"It is highly probable that renewable energy sources could supply the majority of domestic power needs by 2035."
