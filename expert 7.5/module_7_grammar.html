⭐ Band 8.0+ Task 1 Precision Prepositions in Data Trends & Comparisons
Writing Task 1 High Score Essential

Misusing prepositions with data alters factual meaning and reduces your Task Achievement score. Master these four crucial distinctions:

Difference (by)

"increased by 15%"

The margin of increase (from 50% to 65%).

Final Endpoint (to)

"rose to 65%"

The final reached numerical figure.

Noun Margin (of)

"a surge of 2,000 units"

Used after trend nouns (increase, drop, rise).

Precise Point (at)

"peaked at 92% in 2020"

Used with peak, stand, plateau.
