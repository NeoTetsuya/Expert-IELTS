Writing Task 1 Graphs with Future Years

When describing graphs that extend into the future (e.g., up to 2040 or 2050), replace repetitive "will increase" with high-band predictive language:

Projected / Forecast

"Solar energy adoption is projected to climb to 45% by 2040."

Formula: is projected / forecast / anticipated to + Verb

On Track / Set To

"Electric vehicle sales are on track to overtake petrol cars within a decade."

Formula: is set to / is on track to + Verb

Future Perfect Target

"By 2050, the urban population will have doubled in size."

Formula: By [Year], Subject + will have + V3

Writing Task 2 Academic Register

Elevate beyond basic connectors (because, so) by integrating powerful academic causal verbs and phrasal patterns:

Causes Leading to Effects
  • • give rise to / precipitate: "Uncontrolled urban sprawl has given rise to acute traffic congestion."
  • • engender / trigger: "Income inequality frequently engenders social unrest."
Effects Originating from Causes
  • • stem from / originate in: "The current educational deficit stems from prolonged funding cuts."
  • • be attributed to: "The reduction in crime can largely be attributed to community policing."
