Writing Task 1 Academic Range

Instead of stating raw percentages directly, convert numbers into natural academic fractions and proportional qualifiers:

Proportions & Fractions
  • • 73%: "roughly three-quarters"
  • • 32%: "just under a third"
  • • 4%: "a negligible fraction"
Multiplication & Multipliers
  • • x2: "doubled / a twofold rise"
  • • x3: "tripled / a threefold surge"
  • • x4: "quadrupled / fourfold"
Fluctuation & Stability
  • • "hovered around 50%"
  • • "fluctuated marginally between 20-25%"
  • • "plateaued at roughly 80%"
